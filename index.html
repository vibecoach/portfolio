Helping organizations evaluate & articulate possible futures.

From now to new to next, I help organizations turn fragmented ideas into clear strategic direction shared and aligned over teams. From exploring future visions to developing impactful (digital) products pragmatic executable roadmaps. My work bridges business, design and tech, especially where change is needed but the way forward is still unclear. Over the past 25 years I've worked across startups, scale-ups and enterprise organizations on digital products, platforms and transformation initiatives for companies including Philips, Rabobank, NN Group, Schiphol Airport, Monster and HP.
