Side hustles

Staying sharp
by building things.

The best way to stay current with technology is to use it on things that matter to you. These projects are not client work. They are a personal laboratory: part curiosity, part conviction, part vibecoding. Each one started as a question or an observation that wouldn't leave me alone. Some are early concepts. Some are further along. All of them reflect the way I think about problems: name it, frame it, make it feel like it already exists.

Projects
Re:viewer
A vibecoaching platform that matches builders and makers with expert reviewers. Turns the moment of "I think this is ready" into a structured, high-quality feedback loop.
Live
Modulair
An agentic risk negotiator platform for airports. Helps operations teams navigate capacity constraints and conflicting priorities through structured, AI-assisted negotiation between stakeholders.
Agentic AI
Re:framer
An interactive thinking hats and reframing tool. Helps teams and individuals deliberately shift perspective on a problem, structured around proven frameworks for lateral thinking.
Thinking tools
Time/to/x
An event countdown and impact analyser. Not just a timer — a tool that makes the time before a decision or deadline actionable, surfacing what needs to happen and what the stakes are.
Productivity
Off/On
A solution that prevents offsite sessions from derailing and ensures continuity between strategy and planning. Bridges the gap between the energy of an offsite and the reality of Monday morning.
Strategy tools
